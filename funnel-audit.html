What the audit covers

Every part of the funnel,
not just the pretty page.

A funnel does not leak in one place. I check the whole path from first click to paid, so the plan you get fixes the real bottleneck, not the obvious one.

  • Offer clarity: is the thing you are selling obvious in five seconds?
  • Hook and headline: does the first screen earn the scroll?
  • Page flow: every step from entry to conversion, in order.
  • Copy and messaging: does it speak to the buyer or about you?
  • Calls to action: one clear next step per section, or a fork?
  • Friction and form length: every extra field that costs you a lead.
  • Trust and proof: do visitors believe you before you ask for the sale?
  • Mobile experience: where it breaks on the device most people use.
  • Speed and load: the silent killer before anyone reads a word.
  • Tracking: are you even measuring where people drop off?
