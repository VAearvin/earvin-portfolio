The Chef

One kitchen, one menu, one quiet room

Solène began with a small idea. Cook for a few guests each night, with the best of the season, and leave nothing to chance. The dining room seats twenty-six. There is one menu, and the kitchen is open to it.

Our chef works the pass each evening and writes the menu the same morning, around what the markets and the coast offer that day. Nothing sits long. Most of what reaches your table was chosen that morning and finished by hand minutes before.

Before You Arrive

A few quiet details

The full tasting menu is served to the whole table. We do not offer a separate menu, though we are glad to adjust courses for allergies and dietary needs when you tell us in advance.

Yes. Please note any allergies, vegetarian, or other needs when you reserve. Because the menu is written each morning, the more notice we have, the better we can plan a thoughtful version for you.

Plan for about two and a half hours at the table. The menu is paced gently, with time between courses, and there is one seating each night so the room is never rushed.

There is no strict code. Most guests arrive smart and comfortable. Come as you would for an evening you have looked forward to.

The room seats twenty-six, so tables run up to six guests. For a full buyout or a private evening, please reach out by email and we will see what we can arrange.

Because seats are limited, we ask for at least forty-eight hours notice to change or cancel. We hold a card on file at the time of booking and confirm the details with you by email.
