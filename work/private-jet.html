Good to Know

Questions, answered

How far in advance do I need to book? +
For most domestic trips, we can arrange an aircraft with as little as four hours of notice. Longer or international routes are easier with a day or more, but our team will always tell you what is realistic for your trip.
How is the price calculated? +
Your quote depends on the aircraft, the distance, the dates, and airport fees. We give you one all-in number before you confirm, so the price you see is the price you pay.
Are the aircraft safe and well maintained? +
We only charter operators that meet leading third-party safety standards, including ARGUS Platinum and Wyvern Wingman. Every trip is matched to a vetted aircraft and a qualified crew.
What is the difference between on-demand and a Jet Card? +
On-demand means you request a quote each trip with no commitment. A Jet Card lets you prepay flight hours at a fixed, capped rate with guaranteed availability, which suits travelers who fly often.
Can you handle catering and ground transport? +
Yes. Your advisor can arrange cabin catering, ground transport, and special requests as part of the trip. Just let us know what you need when you book.
Do you fly internationally? +
We do. Our heavy and long-range aircraft cover transatlantic and other long routes, and our team handles the customs and permit details so you can simply travel.
