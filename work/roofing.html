Common Questions

Good to know before you start

How long does a roof replacement take? +
Most homes are finished in one to three days, depending on size, pitch, and weather. We give you a clear timeline before any work begins and keep you updated each day.
Do you really offer $0 down financing? +
Yes. Many homeowners qualify for plans with no money down and fixed monthly payments. We walk you through the options and the exact terms during your free estimate, with no obligation.
Can you help with my insurance claim? +
Absolutely. For storm and hail damage we document everything, meet your adjuster on site when needed, and guide you from the first inspection through the final payout.
Are you licensed and insured? +
We are fully licensed and carry both liability and workers coverage. You can ask for our license number and certificate of insurance at any time, and we are happy to share them.
What does the warranty cover? +
Your install comes with a 25-year workmanship warranty in writing. If our labor ever causes an issue, we fix it. Your shingles and materials are also covered by the manufacturer warranty.
Is the estimate really free? +
Yes. The inspection and written estimate cost nothing and come with no pressure. You only move forward if the price and plan feel right to you.
