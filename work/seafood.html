Tide to Table

From the dock to your plate, often in the same morning

We are fifty steps from the water, and that is the whole idea. The boats pull in, we walk down, and the kitchen builds the day's board around what they bring.

  • ✓Daily dockside deliveries. No long supply chain. What you eat tonight was likely swimming this morning.
  • ✓We buy the whole catch. Working with local boats means less waste and a better price for fishers.
  • ✓Shucked and grilled to order. Nothing sits. Oysters are opened when you order them, not before.
  • ✓Simple, honest cooking. Salt, citrus, fire, and butter. We let the fish be the star.

Good to Know

Questions before you come in

How fresh is the seafood, really?

We buy from local boats that dock just steps away, often the same morning we serve it. The Fresh Board is built each day around what comes in, so the catch is about as fresh as a restaurant can offer.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for dinner and weekends, since window tables fill up fast. The raw bar always keeps seats open for walk-ins if you would rather drop by.

Is there anything for people who do not eat seafood?

Yes. We keep a few garden plates, a grilled vegetable bowl, and seasonal sides on hand. Just let your server know and we will take care of you.

Can you handle allergies and shellfish concerns?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book or when you sit down. Our kitchen is happy to guide you to safe choices and adjust plates where we can.

Do you take large groups?

We love a full table. For parties of eight or more, give us a call so we can set the right space and plan a few platters for sharing.

Is there parking near the harbor?

There is a public lot a short walk from the door, plus street parking along the waterfront. Most guests find a spot within a few minutes of arriving.

Come for the oysters, stay for the sunset. Reserve a harborside table and we will have the board fresh and the bar ready.
