The Pour

Ask your server to pair your cut. A fatty ribeye loves a smoky single malt, while a lean filet sits beautifully next to a bold Cabernet. Over 120 whiskeys and 80 wines by the bottle.

The 45-Day Process

Time does most of the work

We bring in whole cuts and age them ourselves in a glass-walled room you can see from the bar. Forty-five days of cold, still air concentrates the flavor and tenderizes the meat. Nothing is rushed.

  • ✓Whole cuts, aged in house. No shortcuts and no shipped-in shortcuts. We do it here.
  • ✓45 days at the bone. The crust we trim away is what makes the inside taste like this.
  • ✓Finished over live coals. Real fire for a clean char and a smoky edge you can taste.
  • ✓Rested, then carved. Every steak rests before it reaches you, so the juice stays in the meat.
