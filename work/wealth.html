The Fee-Only Difference

You deserve advice that puts you first

Many firms earn commissions when you buy a product. We do not. As a fee-only fiduciary, we are paid only by you, so the advice you get is the advice we would give our own family.

  • ✓No commissions, ever. We never earn a hidden cut for selling you a product.
  • ✓Held to the fiduciary standard. We are required to act in your best interest, in writing.
  • ✓Clear, flat pricing. You always know what you pay and exactly what you get.
  • ✓Independent and conflict-free. No quotas, no parent company products to push.
